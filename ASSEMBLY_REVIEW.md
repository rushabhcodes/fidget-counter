The current revision removes the full-cap push action and SW1/R5/C17. A fixed shaft is integral with the stationary base, and the hollow cap rotates on the40×52×7 mm bearing. There are no return springs, sliding carrier, guide fasteners/washers, pusher or elastomer actuator. Ploopy Knob informs the rotation architecture; the Surface Dial press reference is historical.
